About

From population data to public health evidence

I am a biostatistician trained in statistics, biostatistics, and demography. My undergraduate degree in Statistics at Ramadevi Women’s University, Bhubaneswar, gave me a rigorous quantitative foundation, and my M.Sc. at the International Institute for Population Sciences (IIPS), Mumbai, supported by an Institute Fellowship, turned that foundation toward population and health questions.

My research so far has used national datasets, including the National Family Health Survey (NFHS), to study infertility, teenage marriage and pregnancy, antenatal care utilization, and the effect of COVID-19 on life expectancy in India. Across these projects I have worked on identifying the sociodemographic and health factors behind important outcomes and on turning the findings into recommendations for targeted action.

In my current role at Hi-Tech Medical College & Hospital I advise institutional leadership on statistical matters and guide students and researchers in research design and analysis. This work has strengthened my interest in a career centered on epidemiologic research, and I am now applying to doctoral programs in epidemiology to build the methodological depth and independence needed to lead such work.

Research Interests

Where my questions come from

Epidemiology & Public Health Policy

Using population-level evidence to understand disease burden and health inequities, and to inform policy and targeted interventions.

Reproductive & Maternal Health

Infertility and its links to sociodemographic, lifestyle, and non-communicable disease factors; teenage marriage and pregnancy; and inequality in antenatal care utilization.

Mortality & Population Health

Demographic measures such as life expectancy, including the national-level impact of the COVID-19 pandemic in India.

Advanced Statistical Methods

Multilevel modeling, multivariate analysis, and survival analysis applied to health data, with attention to clear data visualization.

Systems Biology & Multidisciplinary Statistics

An interest in connecting statistics with biological systems and with other disciplines to understand health outcomes more completely.
